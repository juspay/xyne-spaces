Viewing message reactions on mobile is now much easier with a new Reactions Drawer. Instead of relying on cramped popovers or hard-to-tap UI, reactions open in a full-width, touch-friendly drawer that clearly shows who reacted and with which emoji—making it effortless to scan engagement on a message.



The drawer experience is optimized for small screens with better spacing and smoother interactions, improving readability and reducing mis-taps. This brings reaction viewing on mobile closer to a native chat experience while keeping the rest of the messaging flow unchanged.
