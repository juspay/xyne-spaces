---

We’ve refreshed dark mode styling across Xyne Spaces to make the experience more consistent, readable, and visually balanced throughout the product. Colors, backgrounds, borders, and text contrast have been tuned across core surfaces—so screens feel cohesive instead of switching between slightly different dark themes.



These updates improve usability in low-light environments by reducing harsh contrast, improving hierarchy, and making interactive states (hover, selected, disabled) clearer. Overall, dark mode now feels more polished and predictable across navigation, content panels, modals, and key workflows.
