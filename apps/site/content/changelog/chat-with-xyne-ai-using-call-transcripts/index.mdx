You can now chat with Xyne AI using your call transcript to quickly understand, summarize, and extract value from meetings. After a call, the transcript becomes available as context for Xyne AI—so you can ask questions like “What decisions did we take?”, “What are the action items and owners?”, “What was the main blocker?”, or “Summarize this in 5 bullets for the ticket update” and get answers grounded in what was actually discussed.



This helps teams reduce manual note-taking and speeds up follow-ups. Instead of replaying recordings or scrolling through long transcripts, you can use Xyne AI to pull out the key moments, convert outcomes into clear next steps, and create structured updates for stakeholders. It’s especially useful for handoffs and async collaboration—anyone can catch up on a meeting by asking the AI for the highlights, decisions, and pending tasks.



By turning transcripts into an interactive knowledge source, Xyne AI makes calls more actionable. The transcript doesn’t just get stored—it becomes searchable, queryable context that you can use to drive ticket progress, write summaries, and ensure nothing important is lost after the meeting ends.
