## Design Context

### Users

Everyday professionals manage their own Digital Twin inside Xyne Spaces. They need to understand
what the Twin knows, review what it wants to remember, inspect where knowledge came from, shape how
it represents them, and see whether background learning is healthy without needing operator-level
knowledge.

### Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, inspectable. The experience should feel like a well-kept personal intelligence
ledger: precise enough to audit, quiet enough to use every day, and candid about uncertainty.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a restrained editorial control-desk aesthetic within Xyne's existing Agent Hub shell. Preserve
Classic, Summer Breeze, and Midnight themes and the product's coral interaction accent. Prefer
neutral surfaces, hairline rules, readable typography, provenance, and asymmetric information
layouts over generic dashboard cards, glass effects, decorative charts, gradients, or neon AI
styling.

### Design Principles

1. Put memories and review decisions before diagnostics.
2. Make provenance, background work, and uncertainty visible in plain language.
3. Use one clear primary action per view and label consequential actions.
4. Flatten hierarchy with spacing and dividers instead of nesting every region in a card.
5. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA with visible focus, keyboard-complete flows, 44px core targets, accessible
   chart alternatives, and reduced-motion behavior.
