We’ve added a new **RCA (Root Cause Analysis) panel** inside **Ticket Details** so teams can document investigation outcomes in a structured, easy-to-scan format—right where the ticket lives. Instead of burying critical context in long comment threads, the RCA panel gives a dedicated space to capture the final “why” and “what changed” in a consistent way.

RCA details are added **manually** using clear fields such as **attributes**, **impact**, **code references/changes**, and **impact type/notes**. This makes it simpler to standardize how RCAs are written across the team, improves handoffs, and helps future readers quickly understand the root cause and blast radius without having to reconstruct the story from scattered messages.
