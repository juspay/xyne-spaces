title: "Automated Stage ETA Reminders"
description: "We’ve added automatic reminders for stage-level ETA breaches to help teams stay on top of ticket progress."
date: "2026-02-19"
---
We’ve added automatic reminders for stage-level ETA breaches to help teams stay on top of ticket progress. A new cron job runs every 30 minutes and sends reminders for tickets whose current stage ETA was breached in the last 30 minutes.



Stage ETA reminders are sent only when the ticket’s overall ETA is not breached and the ticket is in a non-terminal status. Once the overall ETA is breached, the system switches from stage-level reminders to overall ETA reminders to reflect the higher-priority escalation.
