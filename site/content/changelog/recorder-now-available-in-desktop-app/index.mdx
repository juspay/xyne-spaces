The **Recorder** is now enabled in the Xyne Space **desktop app**, so you can capture thoughts, updates without switching to another tool or losing context. Whether you’re in the middle of a ticket investigation, reviewing a thread, or coordinating with the team, you can hit record and quickly save what matters while it’s fresh.

This is especially useful for fast-moving workflows where typing everything out is slow or distracting. You can record key decisions, next steps, customer context, or quick summaries on the go then use those recordings to share accurate updates, reduce back-and-forth, and avoid missing critical details.

By bringing Recorder to desktop, Xyne Space becomes a more complete “capture + communicate” workspace. It helps teams document work as it happens, improve handoffs, and keep conversations aligned—without the friction of jumping between apps or relying on memory later.
