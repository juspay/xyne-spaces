Call hosts now have stronger moderation controls with new mute management features. Hosts can mute an individual participant when needed (e.g., background noise, side conversations, or to keep the discussion focused) and can also use Mute All to quickly bring the room back under control during larger calls.



This update improves call quality and meeting flow, especially in high-participant or noisy environments. These controls give hosts a simple way to maintain clarity during discussions without interrupting the session or asking participants to manually adjust their microphones.
