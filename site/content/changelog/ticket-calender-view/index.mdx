Ticket management now includes a new Calendar view alongside Kanban and Table, letting you visualize tickets by creation date for a more time-based workflow view. The calendar supports Month, Week, and Day modes with a dedicated navigation toolbar for moving across date ranges, and uses compact ticket badges to keep the grid readable. This was added to help teams (especially Marketing) spot ticket creation patterns over time and plan work and resourcing more effectively.
