date: "2026-03-05"
---

Xyne AI outputs now support user tagging, making AI-generated summaries and responses more interactive and actionable. When Xyne AI references people, it can render them as clickable @mentions with hover profiles, so readers can quickly identify and navigate to the right teammates without manual lookup.



For summarizer-style responses, we’ve also added richer participant context by showing participant avatars along with a total participant count. This improves clarity on who was involved, helps teams interpret ownership and relevance faster, and makes AI summaries feel more connected to the real conversation context.
