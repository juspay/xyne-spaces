> along step by step.

## Quick Start

New here? Complete these 3 steps to get up and running in under 5 minutes.

1. **Open Chat → #general** — Say hello to your team and browse channels
2. **Create your first ticket** — Track an issue or task end-to-end
3. **Try Xyne AI** — Ask a question about your workspace

## Messaging

_Channels, direct messages, threads, and notifications_

---

### Chat (Channels)

**Main place for team conversations and updates.** **Find it:** Sidebar → Chat

**Actions:** Open channels · Read and reply to threads · Share files and links

**Steps:**

1. Click "Chat" in the left sidebar to open the channels list.
2. Select a channel — e.g. #general or #engineering — to open the conversation.
3. Type your message in the text box at the bottom and press Enter to send.
4. To reply inside a thread, hover a message and click the reply icon on the
   right.
5. To share a file, click the attach button next to the message box and choose a
   file.
6. Xyne Spaces checks your spelling as you type — red underlines flag
   misspellings. Right-click to see suggestions.
7. Type ASCII emoticons like ":)" or "<3" and they automatically convert to
   emoji (smiley face, heart) when you send.

> **Tip:** Use @ to mention a teammate (e.g. @alice.chen) so they get a
> notification instantly. Typing @ also triggers bot search — if your workspace
> has bots configured, select one to start an automated conversation with it.

---

### Direct Messages

**Private one-to-one or small group conversations.** **Find it:** Chat → DMs tab

**Actions:** Message a teammate · Start private discussions · Share quick
updates

**Steps:**

1. Open Chat from the sidebar, then click the "DMs" tab at the top.
2. A badge on the DMs tab shows your total unread DM count at a glance.
3. Click the create message button to start a new conversation.
4. Type a teammate's name — e.g. "Bob Kumar" — and select them from the
   dropdown.
5. Write your message and press Enter. The conversation is private to you and
   Bob.
6. To start a group DM, click the create message button and add multiple names
   one by one. When more than one person is selected, the form shows "Group
   conversation with N people". Click "Start DM" to create it.

> **Tip:** Star a DM to pin it at the top of your list — great for people you
> message daily.

---

### Threads

**Keep replies organized under the original message.** **Find it:** Chat →
Threads

**Actions:** Open thread inbox · Reply in context · Track active discussions

**Steps:**

1. Go to Chat and click the "Threads" tab in the top navigation — threads are
   available on messages sent in channels.
2. The Threads inbox shows all threads you've participated in or been mentioned
   in.
3. Click any thread entry to jump straight to that conversation in context.
4. Reply inside the thread panel on the right without leaving your current
   channel.

> **Tip:** To get updates on a thread even without replying, hover the message,
> click "More", and select "Subscribe to notifications".

### Bookmarks

**Save important items for quick future access.** **Find it:** Chat → Bookmarks

**Actions:** Save key messages · Organize references · Re-open critical content
· Snooze a bookmark · Mark a bookmark as done

**Steps:**

1. Hover over any message, click the ellipsis options menu (⋮) in the hover
   toolbar, and select "Add bookmark".
2. The message is instantly saved. Open Chat → "Bookmarks" to see all saved
   items.
3. Click a bookmark entry to jump back to the original message in its channel.
4. To remove a bookmark, hover the message, click the ellipsis options menu (⋮)
   in the hover toolbar, and select "Remove bookmark" — or click "Remove"
   directly in the Bookmarks panel.
5. To snooze a bookmark, open the Bookmarks panel, hover a bookmark item, and
   click the snooze button.
6. Choose a snooze duration: 30 mins, 1 hour, 3 hours, Tomorrow at 9:00 AM, or
   Monday at 9:00 AM — the bookmark will resurface at the chosen time.
7. Click the done button on a bookmark entry to mark it as done — it moves to
   the completed section and stops further snooze reminders.

> **Tip:** Snooze a bookmark to "remind me later" — great for messages you
> cannot action right now but do not want to forget.

### Scheduled Messages

**Write now, send later at the right time.** **Find it:** Sidebar → Scheduled
Messages

**Actions:** Create scheduled message · Choose send time · Edit or cancel
pending schedules

**Steps:**

1. Click the "Scheduled Messages" icon in the left sidebar to open the scheduled
   messages page.
2. Click "Create Scheduled Message" in the top-right to open the compose form.
3. Select the target channel, write your message, and choose the days and time
   to send — e.g. "Weekdays (Mon–Fri) at 9:00 AM".
4. Click "Save" to confirm — the message appears in the list with its channel,
   schedule, and send time.
5. Click any scheduled message card to edit the content, change the schedule, or
   cancel it.

> **Tip:** Use the channel and author filters at the top of the page to quickly
> find a specific scheduled message.

### Emoji Reactions

**React to any message with an emoji in one click.** **Find it:** Chat → Hover a
message → Emoji button

**Actions:** Add an emoji reaction · Toggle reaction off · View who reacted

**Steps:**

1. Hover over any message in a channel or DM — the hover toolbar appears on the
   right.
2. Click the emoji button in the toolbar to open the emoji picker.
3. Browse or search for an emoji — e.g. type "thumbs" to find the thumbs-up
   emoji quickly.
4. Click the emoji to add your reaction. It appears below the message with a
   count.
5. Click the same emoji again directly below the message to remove your
   reaction.
6. Hover over a reaction count to see the list of people who reacted.

> **Tip:** Add a checkmark reaction to signal "done" or "acknowledged" without
> sending a reply that clutters the thread.

### User Profiles

**View teammate details, presence, and common channels.** **Find it:** Click any
avatar → Profile

**Actions:** Open a profile · Edit your own profile · Start a DM or call from
profile · Edit display name, team, and phone

**Steps:**

1. Click on any teammate's avatar or name anywhere in the app to open their
   profile panel.
2. The profile shows their name, presence (Online / Away), custom status, email,
   and team.
3. Click "Message" to start a DM directly from the profile panel without
   navigating elsewhere.
4. Click the call button to start a call with that person immediately.
5. To edit your own profile, click your account menu at the bottom of the
   sidebar, then click "Profile". Update your display name, team, and phone
   number, then press Enter to save.

> **Tip:** The "Common Channels" section at the bottom of a profile shows every
> channel you share — great for knowing who to loop in.

### Message Actions

**Act on any message directly from the hover toolbar.** **Find it:** Chat →
Hover any message → toolbar on the right

**Actions:** Create a ticket from a message · Ask AI about a message · Pin, copy
link, or forward a message · Mark as unread or subscribe to a thread · Edit or
delete your own message

**Steps:**

1. Hover over any message to reveal the action toolbar on its right side.
2. Click the emoji icon in the toolbar to add an emoji reaction.
3. Click the reply button to reply in-thread without navigating away from the
   channel.
4. Click "Create Ticket" (ticket icon) to instantly open a new ticket pre-filled
   with the message text — no copy-pasting required.
5. Click the Xyne AI icon to ask the AI assistant a question in the context of
   that specific message.
6. Click the ellipsis menu (⋮) in the toolbar to open additional actions.
7. Click "Add bookmark" to save the message to your Bookmarks.
8. Click "Subscribe to notifications" (bell icon) to add yourself to that
   thread's notification list even without replying.
9. Click "Mark as unread" (dot icon) to reset the channel unread marker to that
   message so you can return to it later.
10. Click "Pin message" (pin icon) to make it visible to everyone via the
    Channel Info → Pinned Messages.
11. Click "Copy link" (link icon) to copy a deep link you can paste anywhere to
    jump directly to that message.
12. Click "Copy message" (copy icon) to copy the plain text of the message.
13. Click "Forward message" (forward icon) to open the Forward Message modal and
    send to another channel or DM.
14. Click "Edit" (edit icon) or "Delete" (trash icon) to modify or remove the
    message (available only for your own messages).

> **Tip:** "Create Ticket from message" is the fastest way to turn a bug report
> or feature idea in chat into a trackable ticket — the message text becomes the
> ticket description automatically.

### Channel Management

**Organise, customise, and control individual channels.** **Find it:** Channel
header → Info button or channel name dropdown

**Actions:** Star a channel · Browse all channels · Set per-channel notification
level · Add members to a channel · Browse channel content tabs · Archive or
leave a channel

**Steps:**

1. Open a channel and click the channel name at the top of the screen to open
   the Channel Info panel.
2. Click the star button in the Channel Info panel to star the channel.
3. To browse all available channels, click the "Browse channels" control (search
   icon) in Chat and search or join any public channel from the list.
4. To change notification settings for this channel only, click the bell icon in
   the channel header and choose: All Messages, Mentions Only, or Nothing.
5. Use the tabs inside the Channel Info panel to browse: About, Members,
   Notifications, and Settings.
6. To add more people, click "Add people" in the channel header or type /invite
   @username in the message box.
7. To leave a channel, open the channel header menu and select "Leave Channel" —
   it is removed from your sidebar but history is preserved.
8. Admins can archive a channel from the same three-dot menu by selecting
   "Archive Channel" — archived channels become read-only and are hidden from
   new members.
9. Channel admins can rename the channel, update its description, or convert it
   between public and private via Channel Settings.

> **Tip:** Star your 3–5 most important channels to keep them pinned at the top
> of the sidebar for instant access without scrolling.

### Forward Message

**Send any message to another channel or DM with optional context.** **Find
it:** Chat → Hover a message → More → Forward Message

**Actions:** Forward a message to a channel · Forward a message to a DM · Add a
note before forwarding

**Steps:**

1. Hover over any message and click the forward button in the hover toolbar to
   open the Forward Message modal.
2. The Forward Message modal opens — the original message is shown in a preview
   at the bottom.
3. Use the search box to type the destination: a channel name (e.g. "#product")
   or a teammate's name for a DM.
4. Select the destination from the dropdown results.
5. Optionally type a note in the text field below the preview — e.g. "FYI — this
   is related to ticket #1024".
6. Click "Forward" — the message appears in the destination with a "Forwarded
   from #source-channel" attribution banner.

> **Tip:** Use Forward instead of copy-paste to preserve the original author
> attribution and include a deep link back to the source message.

### Thread Panel

**Reply, escalate, and act on threads without leaving your channel.** **Find
it:** Chat → Hover a message → Reply in thread → panel opens on the right

**Actions:** Reply in context · Start a call from a thread · Ask Xyne AI about a
thread · Expand thread view · Work with ticket-linked details

**Steps:**

1. Hover a message in a channel and click the reply icon — or click "X replies"
   below a message — to open the Thread Panel on the right. Note: threads are
   only available in channels, not in DMs.
2. Reply in the thread by typing in the text box at the bottom of the panel and
   pressing Enter.
3. Use the navigation tabs at the top of the panel (Messages, Details, Files)
   based on what is available for that thread.
4. In the Details tab, update linked ticket fields (assignee, priority, status,
   tags) without leaving chat.
5. Click the call button in the thread panel header to start a call with the
   other participants in that thread.
6. Click the Xyne AI button in the thread panel header to ask AI to summarise
   the thread or suggest next actions based on the conversation.
7. Click the expand button to open the full thread and ticket detail panel.

> **Tip:** Use the Thread Panel Details tab to update ticket fields inline — it
> is faster than opening the full ticket page when you just need to change
> status or assignee.

### Ticket Thread Panel

**Manage ticket-linked conversations in a dedicated panel.** **Find it:** Open a
ticket-linked thread from Chat

**Actions:** Review ticket context · update ticket fields · navigate ticket
details · access RCA context when present

**Steps:**

1. Open a thread attached to a ticket from a channel conversation.
2. In the thread panel, use the Details area to review and update ticket fields
   like assignee, priority, and status.
3. Use Files and related context blocks to inspect ticket evidence without
   leaving chat.
4. When the ticket includes RCA metadata, review it from the ticket context view
   before escalating or resolving.

> **Tip:** Treat chat thread panel and ticket thread panel as separate
> workflows: chat threads for conversation, ticket threads for execution
> context.

### Channel Rename

**Rename a channel to keep it clear and relevant as the team evolves.** **Find
it:** Chat → Channel Info panel → About section → Channel Name card

**Actions:** Rename an existing channel · Update channel purpose

**Steps:**

1. Open the channel you want to rename by clicking it in the Chat sidebar.
2. Click the channel name in the header to open the Channel Info panel.
3. In the About section, find the "Channel Name" card and click the edit button
   to enter edit mode.
4. Type the new name — channel names are lowercase with hyphens, e.g.
   "platform-engineering".
5. Click "Save" to confirm. All existing message history stays intact; only the
   name changes.

> **Tip:** Notify the team in the channel after renaming — post a quick message
> like "Renamed to #platform-engineering to better reflect our scope."

## Activity & Discovery

_Activity feed, catch-up recaps, and global search_

---

### Activity

**See recent updates, mentions, and important events.** **Find it:** Chat →
Activity

**Actions:** Review recent events · Catch missed notifications · Jump to source
message

**Steps:**

1. Open Chat and click the "Activity" tab in the top navigation.
2. Scan the feed — each row shows who mentioned you or reacted to your message.
3. Click any activity item to jump directly to that message in the original
   channel.
4. Use the filter tabs at the top (Your Mentions, Replies, Reactions, Tickets,
   etc.) to narrow down the activity feed.
5. Mark all visible items as read using the "Mark as read" button at the top
   right of the Activity panel.

> **Tip:** Activity is your quickest way to catch up after being away — faster
> than scrolling channels.

---

### Recap

**Get a summary of what happened while you were away.** **Find it:** Chat →
Recap

**Actions:** Read quick summaries · Catch up faster · Open key linked messages

**Steps:**

1. Click "Recap" in the Chat navigation tabs.
2. The AI-generated summary shows what happened across your channels the
   previous day.
3. Scan the per-channel summaries — e.g. "#engineering had 14 messages about the
   API migration".
4. Click a numbered citation next to a recap point to view the source message in
   context.
5. To change which channels appear in your recap, click the settings icon and
   update your channel selection.

> **Tip:** Run Recap first thing in the morning instead of scrolling every
> channel manually.

### Global Search

**Find messages, tickets, users, and files quickly.** **Find it:** Anywhere →
Command Menu **Shortcut:** `Cmd/Ctrl + K`

**Actions:** Search people and channels · Find old messages · Search bots with @
· Open results instantly

**Steps:**

1. Press Cmd+K (Mac) or Ctrl+K (Windows/Linux) from anywhere in the app.
2. Start typing — e.g. "API timeout" — to search across messages, tickets, and
   users.
3. Use arrow keys to navigate results, then press Enter to open the selected
   item.
4. Prefix your query with "#" to quickly switch to a specific channel by name.
5. Prefix your query with "@" to search bots quickly (for example,
   "@release-bot").
6. When you select a bot result, Xyne opens the bot chat context so you can
   message that bot directly from chat.
7. You can find that bot chat in the DM area under Chat → DMs (it appears like a
   direct-message thread with the bot).
8. Use the tabs at the top (People, Messages, Channels, Tickets, Files, Canvas,
   Calls) to filter results by type.

> **Tip:** Use "#" for channel jump and "@" for bot lookup. Selecting a bot from
> search takes you straight into bot chat, so you can ask it immediately.

---

### Chat with Bots from Search

**Open a bot conversation directly from the command menu.** **Find it:**
Anywhere → Command Menu → Bot result **Shortcut:** `Cmd/Ctrl + K`

**Actions:** Find bots quickly · Open bot chat directly · Start asking
immediately

**Steps:**

1. Press Cmd+K (Mac) or Ctrl+K (Windows/Linux) to open Global Search.
2. Type "@" followed by the bot name — e.g. "@release-bot" — to filter to bot
   results.
3. Use arrow keys to highlight the bot result and press Enter.
4. Xyne opens the bot chat directly so you can send a prompt immediately.
5. If you close the command menu, reopen the same bot chat from Chat → DMs in
   the left sidebar.
6. Type your query in chat and press Enter to start the bot workflow or
   response.

> **Tip:** Use bot search when you already know which automation or assistant
> you need — it is faster than browsing channels first.

## Calls & Collaboration

_Voice and video calls, recordings, canvas, and screen sharing_

---

### Calls

**View and join your recent and scheduled calls.** **Find it:** Sidebar → Calls

**Actions:** Open call history · Rejoin call links · Review call details · Mute
or unmute microphone · Toggle camera · Open in-call chat · Send reactions · Open
call tools · Expand call view · End a call

**Steps:**

1. Click "Calls" in the left sidebar to open the calls list.
2. Your recent calls are listed — tabs show All, Scheduled, and Missed calls.
3. Click "Join call" on an active call link to re-enter the meeting. Clicking on
   participant avatars shows the list of people on the call.
4. To start a new call, click "New Call" and choose "Start an instant call" or
   "Schedule call for later" from the dropdown.
5. While in a call, use the control toolbar at the bottom: click the mic button
   to toggle your microphone on or off.
6. Click the camera button to turn your video on or off.
7. Use the small arrow next to the mic/camera controls to switch your active
   input/output devices when needed.
8. Click the screen share button to share your screen. In the picker that opens,
   choose "Entire Screen" to share everything, or "Window" to share only a
   specific app — a red border appears around the shared content. Click "Stop
   Sharing" when done.
9. Click the participants button to open the participants panel — see who is
   muted, speaking, or waiting. You can also click participant avatars in the
   call to view the people list and profiles.
10. Click the chat button in the call toolbar to open the in-call conversation
    panel and follow live messages.
11. Click the reactions button to send quick emoji feedback without interrupting
    the speaker.
12. Click the tools/apps button to open available call utilities and
    integrations.
13. Click the expand/fullscreen button in the call toolbar to enlarge or focus
    the call view.
14. Click the "Leave call" button to leave the call.
15. To share a call externally, click the share icon in the call header to copy
    the join URL — send it to anyone to let them join even without a Xyne Spaces
    account.

> **Tip:** Mute yourself when you are not speaking — it removes background noise
> for everyone and is good call etiquette.

### My Canvas

**Actions:** Create new canvas · Draft collaborative notes · Reference in chat

## Work Tracking

_Tickets, boards, projects, workflows, and support queues_

---

### Tickets

**Track customer or internal issues from intake to resolution.** **Find it:**
Sidebar → Tickets

**Actions:** Create and assign tickets · Update status · Link ticket discussions

**Steps:**

1. Click "Tickets" in the left sidebar to open the ticket queue.
2. Click "Create Ticket" and fill in the title — e.g. "Bug: API timeout on
   checkout" — and description.
3. Assign the ticket to a teammate using the Assignee dropdown. You can also set
   a Reviewer, QA, and Manager in the create ticket form.
4. Set the priority (Low / Medium / High / Critical) and select a project to
   group it under.
5. Click "Save" — the ticket is created with an ID like #1024 and appears in the
   queue.
6. Update status by clicking the status badge — cycle through Open → In Review →
   Resolved.

> **Tip:** Paste a ticket ID (e.g. #1024) into any chat message — teammates can
> click it to jump directly to the ticket.

---

### Support

**Handle customer issues from triage to resolution.** **Find it:** Sidebar →
Support

**Actions:** Open support queue · Triage incoming requests · Convert to tickets

**Steps:**

1. Click "Support" in the left sidebar to open the support queue.
2. Review incoming requests and open any item to inspect details and
   conversation history.
3. Assign an owner and set priority so urgent requests are handled first.
4. Convert a support request into a ticket when follow-up work is required.
5. Post updates back to the request thread so the reporter can track progress.

> **Tip:** Use Support for intake and communication, and Tickets for structured
> execution.

---

### Projects Board

**Kanban-style board for team execution.** **Find it:** Sidebar → Projects Board

**Actions:** Open project board · Move cards across stages · Track progress

**Steps:**

1. Click "Projects Board" in the left sidebar.
2. Select a project — e.g. "Mobile App v2.0" — from the project list at the top.
3. The board shows columns: Todo, In Progress, Done, Cancelled, and On Hold.
4. Drag a card — e.g. "Design login screen" — from "To Do" to "In Progress" to
   update its status.
5. Click any card to open details: description, assignee, due date, and linked
   tickets.
6. Click "Create Ticket" at the bottom of a column to add a new task directly on
   the board.

> **Tip:** Filter the board by assignee to see only your own cards — use the
> "Assigned to Me" filter in the top bar.

---

### Workflows

**Automate multi-step task execution.** **Find it:** Ticket detail → Workflow

**Actions:** Open workflow runs · Inspect step results · Retry or continue tasks

**Steps:**

1. Open a ticket — e.g. #1024 — and navigate to the Workflow screen for that
   ticket.
2. Use the "Rerun" button to re-trigger the automated steps, or use the workflow
   trigger modal for the initial run.
3. Each step in the workflow shows a status: Pending, Running, Complete, or
   Failed.
4. Click a failed step to see the error log and retry it.
5. Once the workflow completes, click the "Final Git Diff" tab to inspect all
   code changes generated or applied during the run.
6. The "Live Edits" tab shows real-time in-progress changes as the workflow
   executes — no need to refresh.
7. The "Preview Changes" tab lets you review staged changes before they are
   applied.
8. Some workflows include an "RCA" tab — if triggered, it auto-populates the
   Root Cause Analysis form with timeline data from the workflow run.
9. To build a new workflow, go to Settings → Workflows and define the steps.

> **Tip:** Workflows can auto-assign tickets, send notifications, and call
> external APIs — all without code.

---

### My Tickets

**Your personal Kanban board showing only tickets assigned to you.** **Find
it:** Sidebar → My Tickets

**Actions:** See all your assigned tickets · Update ticket status · Plan daily
work

**Steps:**

1. Click "My Tickets" in the left sidebar (or from the Tickets directory) to
   open your personal board.
2. The board shows only tickets assigned to you, organized by status column.
3. Drag a ticket card across columns — e.g. from "In Progress" to "In Review" —
   to update its status.
4. Click any card to open the full ticket detail panel on the right.
5. Use the priority filter in the toolbar to surface Critical and High tickets
   first.

> **Tip:** Start each morning by opening My Tickets — it's your single source of
> truth for what to work on today.

---

### Ticket Detail

**Full ticket view with sub-tickets, related tickets, and attachments.** **Find
it:** Tickets list or Kanban board → Click any ticket

**Actions:** Add sub-tickets · Link related items

**Steps:**

1. Click any ticket in the list or board to open the full detail panel.
2. Set the assignee using the "Assignee" dropdown.
3. Add tags by clicking the "+ Tag" button and typing from the project-wide tag
   list.
4. Scroll to "Sub-Tickets" and click "Create Sub-Ticket" to create a child task
   linked to this ticket.
5. In "Related Tickets", click "+ Add ticket" to link related tickets with a
   relation type (e.g. "Linked", "Possible duplicate", "Duplicate").
6. The Activity tab at the bottom shows a full audit trail: every status change,
   comment, and assignment change.

> **Tip:** Sub-tickets are ideal for breaking down a large feature ticket — the
> parent ticket tracks overall progress automatically.

---

### Ticket Board Views

**Switch between Kanban, Table, and Calendar layouts.** **Find it:** Projects
Board or My Tickets → View switcher (top-right toolbar)

**Actions:** Switch view · Use calendar view · Apply column filters

**Steps:**

1. Open any project board or My Tickets.
2. Click the view switcher icons in the top-right toolbar to switch between
   Kanban, Table, or Calendar view.
3. In Kanban view, drag cards across stages.
4. In Table view, you can bulk-edit multiple tickets at once by selecting
   checkboxes on the left.
5. In Calendar view, tickets with ETAs appear on their due date on the calendar
   grid.
6. Use the filter icon in the toolbar to filter tickets by status, priority,
   assignee, tags, or any dynamic form field.

> **Tip:** Calendar view is the fastest way to spot scheduling conflicts across
> your project — try it before sprint planning.

### Duplicate Ticket Detection

**Automatically prevents duplicate tickets before they are created.** **Find
it:** Tickets → Create Ticket

**Actions:** Prevent duplicate tickets · Review auto-flagged duplicates

**Steps:**

1. Start creating a ticket by clicking "Create Ticket" and filling in the title.
2. Xyne Spaces automatically checks for similar existing tickets in the
   background as you type — no separate action needed.
3. If a potential duplicate is detected early, you will be notified with a flag
   before the ticket is created — review the flagged similar ticket and decide
   whether to proceed or use the existing one instead.

> **Tip:** Duplicate detection runs automatically in the background — the system
> flags potential duplicates early so your team avoids working on the same issue
> twice.

### Xyne AI

**Context-aware AI assistant for day-to-day work.** **Find it:** Top-right

**Actions:** Ask questions · Summarize context · Generate next actions · Start a
new chat session · Browse chat history · Toggle web search or deep research ·
Add context to a query · Attach files · Stop AI response

**Steps:**

1. Click the Xyne AI icon in the top-right action bar to open the AI panel.
2. Type a question in natural language — e.g. "Summarize what happened in
   #incidents today".
3. Xyne AI pulls from your channels, tickets, and knowledge base to give a

4. Ask follow-up questions in the same thread — e.g. "Who is working on it?".

   team.

7. Click the history button to browse your previous AI chat sessions and reopen
   any of them.

   delete what Xyne AI remembers about your workspace.
9. Click the activity button to see a log of your recent AI interactions.
10. Click the settings button to open the Settings modal — it contains two tabs:
    "Custom Instructions" (to shape how Xyne AI responds) and "Skills".
11. Click the close button in the header to close the AI panel without losing
    your current conversation.

17. Click the stop button while the AI is responding to halt generation
    immediately.
18. Xyne AI uses Memory v2 — it remembers key facts about your workspace across
    sessions. Open the Memories panel to review or edit what it knows.

> **Tip:** Use the Custom Instructions tab (inside Settings) to tell Xyne AI

### Group Assignment Config

**Define who can assign and manage tickets within a user group.** **Find it:**
User Groups → Select a group → Auto Assignment → Assignment Configuration

**Actions:** Set manager roles · Configure team lead permissions · Control
ticket routing rules

**Steps:**

1. Click "User Groups" in the left sidebar and open the group you want to
   configure. (Note: this page is accessible to Admins only.)
2. On the selected group page, click the "Auto Assignment" button, then click
   ">" to open "Assignment Configuration".
